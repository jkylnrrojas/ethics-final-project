Option 2: Restricted Companions

Exploring the ethical arguments for unrestricted AI companionship.

Overview

AI companions should have restrictions in place, but still be allowed to simulate deep emotional connections with users. The pros of this are that it would enhance the protections for users and reduce the chance of over-reliance on the model that could lead to negative outcomes. The cons of this are that it would limit the way users interact with the companion, which might affect business for the companies and developers. In terms of stakeholders, users would have safer interactions even if users feel they are limited in those interactions, hosts would have a larger safety margin legally speaking in case of negative outcomes, and regulators would have an easier time regulating this technology if safety measures are already in place.

The Rights Lens Evalutation

This respects the rights of all stakeholders, as regulators get to do their jobs, hosts get to still put out their AI companions and profit from them (they’re just more restricted), and users can still freely use a product without compromising their safety.

The Utilitarian Lens Evaluation

This would do the most good for all stakeholders involved as the safety of users would not be at risk, hosts get to still operate, and regulators aren’t restricted.

The Communitarian Lens Evaluation

This option would serve the community as a whole in that it would protect safety and set standards that would keep people in the community in check.
