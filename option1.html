Option 1: No Restrictions

Exploring the ethical arguments for unrestricted AI companionship.

Overview

AI companions should have no restrictions in order to simulate better emotional bonds. The pros of this are that it’ll be more engaging for users and benefit the investors and developers of the AI companion financially. The cons of this are that it’s easier for users to emotionally rely on the AI instead of real-world connections. In terms of stakeholders, users will have a better experience but could potentially experience a dependency on the AI companion, hosts would benefit in business but could experience harm in their public relations if their model negatively influences users, and regulators would probably be forced to intervene more by the public and enforce public safety.

The Rights Lens Evalutation

This would not respect the user’s right to safety, not respect the regulator’s right to intervene and regulate, but would respect the host’s right to not regulate their platform.

The Utilitarian Lens Evaluation

This would provide the most good for the hosts, but would harm the users and those around them.

The Communitarian Lens Evaluation

This option doesn't serve the community as the effects it could have on users forming relationships among others in their communities is concerning and would not serve the community as a whole overall.
