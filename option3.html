Option 3: Complete Ban

Exploring the ethical arguments for unrestricted AI companionship.

Overview

AI should only be used for function, so AI companions should not be allowed overall. The pros of this are that it would get rid of any ethical risks and it’d be easier to regulate and manage. The cons of this are that it would take away an easily accessible way for people to manage their loneliness and it’d probably lead to unregulated models popping up. In terms of stakeholders, users would technically be safer from this technology but would lose a possible resource in managing their loneliness, hosts would lose out on their product and essentially their stance in the marketplace, and regulators would not have to manage the technology overall.

The Rights Lens Evalutation

This would not respect the host’s right to operate, not respect the user’s right to engage in whatever technology they wish to use, but would respect the regulator’s right to keep this technology in check.

The Utilitarian Lens Evaluation

This option reduces safety risks, but does take away an accessible resource for lonely people, which could cause harm to users.

The Communitarian Lens Evaluation

This option doesn't serve the community as a whole as the whole community loses this technology, which impacts not only users who have a need for it, but also those around them.
